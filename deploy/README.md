# Coming Undone

simply split.

Split a song into parts and turn each one into MIDI:
https://swwallowws.github.io/coming-undone/

This is the built site, published from https://github.com/swwallowws/coming-undone
by its CI. It runs a song online (a Hugging Face Space), on your own computer, or
entirely in your browser, where nothing is uploaded. The guided demo is at /try/.
